## Codebase Patterns
- Routes are defined in `src/App.jsx` using react-router-dom `<Routes>/<Route>` inside a `<BrowserRouter>`
- Page components live in `src/pages/` and are imported directly into `App.jsx`
- The catch-all `<Route path="*">` for NotFound must always be last in the Routes list
