## Codebase Patterns
- Pages in src/pages/ are simple functional components with default exports, no TypeScript
- App.jsx handles routing via react-router-dom (BrowserRouter + Routes + Route)
- Flexbox centering (display: flex, justifyContent: center, alignItems: center) is applied at the App.jsx contentStyle level, not in individual pages
- Lint (eslint) and build (vite build) are the quality checks — no separate typecheck script
- No test script configured; build verification is sufficient
